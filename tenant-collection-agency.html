Tenant Collection Agency for Property Portfolios

A tenant collection agency recovers what residents owe under the lease — a current balance that's fallen behind, debt still open long after the tenant is gone, or an aged account your team stopped chasing — without turning recovery into a legal or reputational liability.

Tenant debt doesn't stop at unpaid rent.

Every tenant account Elite Recovery Group (ERG) takes on falls into one of four stages of the tenant lifecycle.

Active-lease delinquency

A current resident falling behind on rent or recurring fees.

Post-move-out balance

What's left owing once a tenant vacates, including damages and cleaning charges.

Eviction-related debt

Legal and court costs from an eviction that were never recovered.

Aged tenant accounts

Older balances a portfolio quietly wrote off.

A tenant debt collection agency that runs every account through the same script gets the same result regardless of which stage it's in — slow recovery and resident friction. ERG's process is built stage-by-stage instead.
