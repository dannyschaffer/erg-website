Rental collection services can mean two different things: software that processes rent while it's still on time, or a service that recovers rent after it's already gone unpaid. Frequently asked

Rental collection services questions, answered.

  • What's the difference between rental collection services and rent-payment software?

    Payment software automates collecting rent that's still current. ERG's rental collection services start after that: recovering balances already past due, under FDCPA and Regulation F compliance.

  • Does ERG collect on-time rent payments, or only overdue balances?

    Only overdue and delinquent balances. ERG is a recovery specialist, not a payment processor, and works alongside a portfolio's existing payment software rather than replacing it.

  • What debt do ERG's rental collection services cover?

    Resident balances, move-out debt, eviction costs, and aging accounts, the same account types covered under rental debt collection.

  • What states can ERG provide rental collection services in?

    AZ, OR, TX, UT, and WA today, with four more states in progress toward the 2026 expansion.
