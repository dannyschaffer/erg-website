Frequently asked

Property management collections questions, answered.

  • What portfolio types does ERG work with?

    Multifamily, student housing, single-family, and commercial properties — each handled with strategy and outreach adapted to that portfolio type, not a one-size-fits-all process.

  • Does ERG replace our property management team or software?

    No. ERG works alongside internal teams and existing systems as a specialist recovery partner, so property managers can stay focused on leasing, maintenance, and resident experience.

  • What's included in property management collections?

    Resident balances, move-out debt, eviction costs, and aging receivables — the full range of debt a property management portfolio accumulates, not unpaid rent alone.
